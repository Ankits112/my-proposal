Chapter 02

Our Adventures & The Mandatory Trip Fights

Gokarna • United Kingdom • Dubai • Manali

Moving forward into 2023 and 2024, these were easily the best years for us trip-wise (with so many more to come!). We explored Gokarna, crossed over to the United Kingdom for our very first international trip (fyi: went before Paramveer!), marveled at Dubai, and stepped into snowy Manali in 2025.

Can you guess what was truly special about all these trips?
It was YOU.

I loved every single element and emotion of you during our travels: the pure excitement, the endless itinerary planning, the shopping sprees, watching videos together, and yes... our fights!

😅

The Honest Truth: I believe we have fought at least once during every single trip. And every time you get angry, I get angry at that—I know, it’s the most annoying thing about me! But you also know I cool down pretty fast, and I’ve got a 100% record of making you un-angry. :)

"A few unforgettable non-food memories..."

  • 🚗 That scenic, winding drive up to Mukteshwar Temple.
